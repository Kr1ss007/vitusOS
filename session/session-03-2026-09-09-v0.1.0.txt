1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROGRESSION OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 03 establishes both the virtualization test infrastructure and the complete
bare-metal zero-flicker boot-to-shutdown architecture for vitusOS.

vitusOS is fundamentally not an ordinary Linux distribution. The design mandate
is absolute visual and behavioral purity:
  - Zero text console bleed on the physical display from UEFI to power-off.
  - Zero TTY logins or systemd status banners.
  - Zero mode-switching flash, flickering, or display stutter.
  - Zero Plymouth: complete ownership of the display belongs to AnimusBoot,
    animus-early, and the AnimusEngine compositor.
  - Zero kernel panics or crash leaks.
  - Zero mocks, stubs, placeholders, or fake simulations: 100% real Linux kernel APIs.

  - Terminal emulator uses real non-blocking PTY master stream.
  - Hardware storage scans real sysfs block trees and mounts.
